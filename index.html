About

Held-out data decides.

I study Electronics and Communication Engineering at MVJ College of Engineering, Bengaluru (GPA 9.00/10), and co-founded Aušra, where I build agentic AI that automates document-heavy regulatory and safety workflows in life sciences. My research is in clinical machine learning: neonatal sepsis detection, multi-omic cancer classification, and a systematic review and meta-analysis of AI for early sepsis detection, under review at the Journal of Biomedical Informatics.

  • 01

    Select on held-out data

    Final models are chosen on validated performance, not training-set fit — cross-validation, golden datasets, and CI regression gates that catch drift before release.

  • 02

    Keep a human in the loop

    Regulated work needs audit-ready output. Review layers and human adjudication are designed into the system, not added after it ships.

  • 03

    Make predictions explainable

    SHAP attributions and calibrated uncertainty estimates, so a clinician can see what drove a prediction and how far to trust it.

  • 04

    Build with the people who use it

    Requirements and pilot feedback come straight from users — from hospital-integration pipelines to the front desk of a local dental clinic.
